10 / METHODOLOGY & FINDINGS

Dataset Provenance, Warehouse Design & Governance

Complete documentation of data engineering, star-schema dimensional modeling, OLAP methodology, and machine learning governance.

1. Dataset Provenance & Synthetic Nature ▾

The dataset includes 5,000 synthetic Indian retail loan applications and 56,447 monthly repayment observations. In accordance with regulatory integrity standards, all borrower identities, pan cards, bureau records, and repayment histories are synthetically synthesized for data mining and warehousing coursework. No proprietary customer or credit bureau (CIBIL/Equifax) data was harvested.

2. Data Cleansing & Feature Engineering ▾

The automated ETL pipeline in src/ln/etl_loans.py normalises incoming column aliases, enforces non-negative value constraints, verifies mandatory fields, and calculates key derived metrics: loan_to_income, asset_to_loan, and credit/income band categorisations. Defective records are quarantined into reports/quarantine.csv.

3. Star-Schema vs Snowflake Warehouse Design ▾

The warehouse implements a Star-Schema centered on fact_loan_application, supported by conformed lookup dimensions (dim_region, dim_loan_product, dim_credit_band, dim_education, dim_employment_type, dim_status). A separate accumulating snapshot fact table fact_loan_repayment records monthly instalment schedules. Indexes on status and credit bands ensure sub-second analytical queries.

4. Machine Learning & Leakage Prevention ▾

The modeling pipeline in src/ln/mining.py rigorously excludes post-decision and leakage attributes (e.g. sanctioned_amount_inr, interest_rate_pct, emi_inr, decision_reason, disbursement_date). Models are fitted using stratified 70/15/15 train-validation-test partitions. Models are evaluated across validation F1 scores to determine the selected best model (Logistic Regression), with feature importance derived from model coefficients and calibrated on held-out records.

5. Ethical Lending & Limitations ▾

Automated algorithms can perpetuate proxy biases. Demographic variables such as education and geographic zones must not be utilized as solitary determinants of credit worthiness. Statistical correlations do not prove causality. These analytical findings serve as illustrative decision-support demonstrations only.
